## Heater
[[Heaters no insulation.jpg]]

Physically, the band heaters are just a clamp around the pipe. The mat heaters are like big pieces of rubber that we wrap and secure around the piping.
- All of the heaters are located on the bottom leg of the pipe.
- all of the heaters are being supplied power during each experiment
	- Same V source (in theory)
band heaters
- helped to fill the gaps in distance. We wanted to have 40 inches of a heated section on the bottom but could only find mat heaters in lengths of 6-12 inches. The band heaters are 1 inch wide. four-1 inch band heaters and three-12 inch mat heaters got us to 40 inches of heated section while also being able to supply at least 3000 W of power to the loop
