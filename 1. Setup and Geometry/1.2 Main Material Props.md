## Material-property citations and provenance — November 4, 2026

Cite the publications or theses below for the equations they actually support.
This note and the internal material libraries are discovery/provenance records,
not scholarly references. Keep installed material/geometry information separate
from a model's selected property package.

| Property package or law | Citation and support | Important distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Working HITEC density, constant heat capacity, conductivity and two-term exponential viscosity | Fierro López, master's thesis, chapter 4, “Fixed fluid and solid properties.”[^mat-fierro] | Documents the adopted reduced-model equations. The exact original Jin-paper coefficients have not been independently verified. |
| Legacy salt-loop steel density, heat capacity, conductivity and emissivity | Fierro López, same chapter.[^mat-fierro] | Full implemented set is documented there. IAEA independently supplies the exact conductivity law.[^mat-iaea] |
| Legacy ceramic conductivity and emissivity | Fierro López, same chapter.[^mat-fierro] | Conductivity matches to displayed rounding. Model density 128 kg/m³ and heat capacity 1000 J/(kg K) remain prescribed inputs. |
