\subsection{Framework contribution}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Develop and assess a CFD--experiment order-reduction framework; the resulting model is a fast, interpretable physics-based ROM. [R01; October 14 approved directions]
\item Use the custom 1D solver as the main model now; CFD and experiments inform reduction, closure selection, learning, and assessment. [November 2 author direction; N09]
\item Proposed organizing aims: (1) reduction and development-informed closures; (2) learning and observation-consistent temperature maps; (3) independent assessment and applicability. Refine these sentences with the author. [literature B Section 11]
\item Organizing question: what information and reduced physics are needed to reproduce observed temperatures and then predict new conditions with defensible uncertainty? Keep the development-case demonstration distinct from the independent predictive test. [AD-20261102-02; Review A Sections 1, 10--11]
\item For each aim specify one output, the permitted inputs, a comparator and an evidence product; organize by scientific question rather than by software package or a sequence of paper summaries. [G01: Theory motivation; Review B Section 11]
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Approved staged goals}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Stage 1: steady heat removal/losses, circulation, temperature extrema and supported operating margins. [R01--R02]
\item Stage 2: transitions between operating conditions, thermal/hydraulic response, settling, and time-dependent margins. Stage approval is distinct from implementation readiness. [R01--R02]
\item Stage 3: operational assistance and operator-facing model/data dashboard; collaborate with Ben Booth and NeutronOS. Controls and unattended operation need a separate agreed scope. [R01--R02]
\item Uncertainty estimation and propagation are core across all stages. The older outline's blanket optional-transient/dashboard wording is superseded by R01/R02.
\item Immediate feasibility emphasis is experimental-data interpretation and temperature reproduction, leaning on the master\textquotesingle{}s work. This does not remove approved transition/operator goals or establish their minimum dissertation demonstration. [AD-20261102-02]
\end{itemize}
