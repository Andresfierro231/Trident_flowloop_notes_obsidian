## How to use an answer

- Start with a proposal/research need; locate the corresponding original question.
- Read the existing answer and cited source/dossier; identify its assumptions and unresolved empirical/design work.
- Reconcile with current author direction, experiment/modeling reports and source versions.
- Enrich the relevant proposal bullet/sub-bullet with the answer, primary citation and exact supporting pointer.
- Track the remaining research task separately. “Answered in the review” does not mean a method was implemented, a comparison passed, or a proposal bullet was integrated.
