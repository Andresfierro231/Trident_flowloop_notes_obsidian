Each of the above equations are evaluated using T in Kelvin. The equation for density
comes from Janz et al. [26] and has a 2% uncertainty within the applicable temperature
range of 470-870 K. The equation for dynamic viscosity comes from Jin et al. [50] and has
a 2.5% uncertainty within the applicable temperature range of 420-695 K. The equation for
specific heat capacity provided by Parida et al. [51] does not have a quantified uncertainty,
but does report the specific heat range found within the literature they found. Using that as
the bounds for uncertainty, the uncertainty is approximately ±4% for the liquid phase and is
weakly dependent on temperature, so has been modeled as a constant property. The equation
for thermal conductivity comes from Santini et al. [48] and has an reported uncertainty and
an applicable temperature range of 420-770 K.
