Chapter 5
Computational Modeling
This chapter describes the two computational models developed for this thesis: a first-order
energy balance model for closing the experimental energy budget, and the three-dimensional
OpenFOAM CFD simulation of the full loop.
5.1
First-Order Energy Balance Model
5.1.1
Purpose and Approach
As seen in Chapter 4, there is considerable uncertainty with respect to the global energy
balance of the experimental facility. Lacking information such as detailed insulation config-
urations, external thermal imaging, or insulation temperature measurements, the first step
that needed to be taken in order to model the system was to improve our understanding
of the energy flow into and out of the system. Before tackling that though, it was deemed
prudent to ensure the geometry of the system was well understood as while the publication
lists some helpful dimensions, they are insufficient for simulated reproduction of the exper-
imental results. The exact construct of the facility was not available; neither a computer
model, detailed dimensions, nor Bill of Materials could be provided. Furthermore, the in-
strumentation diagram did not align with as-constructed images of the experimental facility.
