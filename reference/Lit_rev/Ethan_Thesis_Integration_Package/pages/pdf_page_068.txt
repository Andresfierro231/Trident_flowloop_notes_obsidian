5.1.3
Cooler Heat Removal
The cooling power is computed using an energy balance on the air side:
Qcooler = ˙mair [cp(Tout)Tout −cp(Tin)Tin] ,
(5.6)
where cp(T) is evaluated separately at inlet and outlet temperatures using polynomial fits
for air.
This enthalpy balance approach is superior to the average-temperature method
(Q = ˙m¯cp∆T) used in the original experimental analysis, as it correctly accounts for the
nonlinearity of cp(T) over the large air temperature rise (e.g., 25 ◦C to 113 ◦C for salt test
