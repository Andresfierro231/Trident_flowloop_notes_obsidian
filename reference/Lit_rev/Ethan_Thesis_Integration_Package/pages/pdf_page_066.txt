5.1.2
Radial Conduction Solver
For each segment, the radial conduction solver accepts a LoopSegment object defined with
a unique name, segment length, and inner radius, followed by an arbitrary amount of
RadialLayer objects.
Those RadialLayer objects are defined by a material, thickness,
and power for segments such as the primary heater on the lower leg and test section that
are electrically heated during operation.
LoopSegment objects themselves are purely annular models, lacking an internal fluid
volume and associated internal convective heat transfer coefficient. This approach was chosen
for two main reasons, those being: first, to avoid using standard correlations of fully or
partially developed laminar flow while prilimiary calculations showed that the both the
hydrodynamic and thermal entry lengths of both fluids was near or exceeding the total path
length around the loop, in some cases exceeding by several times the loop’s perimeter, as
