For steady-state simulation, the full
CHT approach can be replaced by a mixed (Robin) boundary condition that represents the

Verification and Validation
The credibility of the results in this thesis depend upon the V&V framework applied to it.
Roache [37] is one of the foundational texts for V&V, making the distinction between verifi-
cation, which determines if the intended equations are being solved properly, and validation,
determining if the correct equations are being solved. Oberkampf and Roy [38] extended the
framework into uncertainty quantification and distinguishing between irreducible uncertain-
ties and reducable uncertainties, which must be treated differently and ultimately factor in
to how we determine if the results of this work can be considered validated, and if so, with
what uncertainties attached to it?
