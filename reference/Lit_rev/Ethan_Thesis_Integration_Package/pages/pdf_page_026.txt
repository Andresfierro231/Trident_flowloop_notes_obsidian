lation, the buoyancy head is balanced by the frictional losses integrated along the loop’s
path. From that idea, works such as Vijayan and Austregesilo [18] developed generalized
scaling laws spanning multiple flow regimes and Vijayan later also published trends of sta-
bility for NCLs [19]. Analyses such as these are established workhorses of loop design and
