5. Created a model capable of serving as a suitable starting point for further reduced-order
models and machine learning training with quantified uncertainty validated within a
99% confidence interval.

Future Work
Future work for this project could include any or all of the following aspects:
1. Extensive turbulence analysis using other RANS models to create a detailed account
of the modeling choice’s impact on the previously validated simulations and/or ex-
pansion of the turbulence study to Large Eddy Simulation for higher-resolution of the
development of turbulence within the system.
2. Detailed transient analysis beyond the steady-state modeling performed in this work.
3. Further improvements using reduced-order techniques to further reduce uncertainty
and improve the quality of the model with potential applicability towards true measure-
ment predictions rather than simulated predictions performed far after the experiment
was conducted.
4. Implementation of the validated model into a Digital Twin framework, potentially
serving as feedback to inform future experimental design.
