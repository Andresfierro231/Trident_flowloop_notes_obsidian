• Chapter 4 describes the experimental facility that was used in the foundational work
for this thesis.
In this chapter, the relevant procedures, instrumentation, working
fluids, experimental test matrix, and results will be discussed. Within this chapter,
caveats, along with any other relevant disclaimers discovered during the conducting of
the work within this thesis will be addressed.
• Chapter 5 details the computational modeling approach at each step throughout the
pipeline. This section proceeds stepwise through the pipeline to explain the approach
that was taken to create the first order model and attempt to close the energy balance of
the system. From here, the details of the mesh and its generation are expounded upon,
followed by the numerical schemes and solution settings used within the OpenFOAM
simulation for each case. Next, the actual conduction of the simulation and utilization
of high-performance computing (HPC) resources was performed in addition to the
methodology for collecting the quantities of interest before finally postprocessing the
results and refining the results into easy-to-digest figures to easily determine how well
simulation aligns with the experimental result, along with any methodology corrections
to data collection.
• Chapter 6 presents the results of the pipeline. Within this chapter, the verification of
the methodology is completed and demonstrated for the custom boundary condition
code, grid convergence studies, and parallel scaling performance across mesh refine-
ment levels. Once the verification of the pipeline has been conducted and sufficiently
demonstrated, the simulated results will be compared against the experimental results
to demonstrate to what extent the results of the simulation pipeline can be considered
properly validated.
