5.1.5
Energy Balance Results
For each test case, the energy balance takes the form:
Qheater + Qtest section −Qcooler −Qpassive −Qfins = Qunaccounted,
(5.7)
where Qpassive is the total convection and radiation loss from the each of the pipe segments,
and Qfins is the total fin loss.
The first order model solves each of the 8 experimental cases at once to verify the model’s
applicability to the test matrix. Initially, this universal approach shared insulation configura-
tions between all 8 cases as there are no are no mentions of changes in insulation being done
between the performance of the water and the salt cases. Under this constraint, the global
energy balance of both the water and salt cases could not be acceptably closed simultane-
ously, leading to the conclusion that there must have been different insulation configurations
between the water suite and salt suite of experiments, which was subsequently confirmed by
the experimentalists.
With that confirmation, and the use of two different insulation thicknesses, the model
achieves acceptable energy closure for all test cases, with the exception of salt test 1. The
anamoloies regarding salt test 1 will be further elaborated on in Chapter 6.

OpenFOAM Simulation
5.2.1
Governing Equations
The simulations solve the steady-state incompressible Navier-Stokes equations with buoyancy
and temperature-dependent properties. The continuity, momentum, and energy equations
in the PIMPLE (merged PISO-SIMPLE) framework are: conservation of mass, momentum,
and energy.
