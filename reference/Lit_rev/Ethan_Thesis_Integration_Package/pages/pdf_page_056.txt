From Table 4.4, two main items reveal themselves. First, when applied to the whole
test matrix instead of just the highest power cases for both working fluids (as done in [9]),
Equation 4.7 produces values exceeding the total heater power applied to the system. Second,
the equation used to determine the uncertainty for the net heat does not factor in either
the individual thermocouple uncertainty or their own reported uncertainty for temperature
differences of 1.56 K [9]. Comparing against the values for ∆T in Table 4.4, this unpropagated
uncertainty more than doubles the uncertainty for water cases 1, 2, and 3. This demonstrates
that the need for better energy-balance understanding mentioned in Chapter 1 is even greater
than previously thought.
