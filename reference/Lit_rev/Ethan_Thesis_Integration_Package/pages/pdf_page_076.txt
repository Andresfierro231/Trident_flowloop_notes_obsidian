5.3.5
Bend Segments
The loop contains four pipe bends (two in the lower heater leg, two in the upper cooler leg).
These bends are constructed using Lofts to first connect one face to another and create the
blocks in between before being assigned Arc edges to control the radius of curvature, which
uses a value of 1 inch.
5.3.6
Expanded Cross-Sections
Junction extensions and valve bodies have an expanded outer diameter (12.7 mm radius vs.
the standard 11.811 mm radius). Eight additional concentric ring blocks extend the O-grid
from the bore to the expanded radius, with the annular step face patched as a wall. The
mesh remains fully conformal at these transitions.
5.4
Boundary Conditions
5.4.1
Velocity and Pressure
The loop is a closed domain with no inlet or outlet, complicating the set-up of the simulation
with the lack of an inlet or an outlet.
• Velocity (U): No-slip (noSlip) at all wall patches.
• Pressure (prgh): calculated, the absolute pressure is given an initial value of atmo-
spheric pressure at 101325 Pascals and is calculated at the boundary as the simulation
evolves.
• Hyrdostatic Pressure (prgh): fixedFluxPressure at walls, with the reference pres-
sure set to zero at a point in the downcomer (right vertical leg) at mid-height, away
from thermal and flow disturbances. As the system is closed, this is a guage pressure.
