To ensure that the 3D CAD’s apparent matching to the image of the experimental facility
was not an artifact of poor image scaling, the image was scaled into physical dimensions based
on pixel measurements between known points, primarily relying on the distance from the
center-faces of the right-side fittings. This primary reference was chosen based on having
the exact fittings known, combined with the comparably unremarkable right leg, nominally
being a stock, unbent, 3 ft pipe that was assumed to be fully mated within both fittings. It
is also worth mentioning that the pairs of fittings on both the left and right sides are not
pixel-perfectly aligned vertically, hinting at either slight bending in the straight legs, lens
distortion within the original image, or a combination of both, but this misalignment was
no greater than a single-digit number of pixels for either side and is neglected.
