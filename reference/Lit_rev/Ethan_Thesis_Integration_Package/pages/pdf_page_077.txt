Temperature: rcExternalTemperature
A custom boundary condition, rcExternalTemperature, was developed to model the ther-
mal resistance of cylindrical pipe walls with external convection and radiation. This replaces
OpenFOAM’s stock externalTemperature boundary condition, which assumes a slab ge-
ometry for the wall conduction resistance.
For the TAMU loop, the thickly insulated pipes can have an outer-to-inner radius ratio
in excess of 5, making the slab approximation inaccurate.
