4.5.3
Velocity Data Extraction
Rather than being satisfied with only the full-field salt data and giving a best-guess at the
performance of the simulated water cases, the extraction of three distinct velocity profile
heights of Y/H = 0.25, 0.50, and 0.75 in Figure 14 of Reis et al.
[9] was inspired by
the experience of performing image pixel-measurements for the modeling effort described in
Chapter 5.
Source Figures and Axis Calibration
First, Figure 12 and 14 from Reis et al. [9] were zoomed in on as much as possible to fully
capture one of the four subfigures for each figure in-frame. From there, the "source" image
is turned into a "calibration" image to ensure that the axes and origin for the figure are
set correctly along side the alignment of vertical and horizontal gridlines with the major
tickmarks along the x and y axes.
