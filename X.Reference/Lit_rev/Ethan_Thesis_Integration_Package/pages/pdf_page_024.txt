Chapter 2
Literature Review
This chapter reviews literature relevant to the present work. Topics covered are: the funda-
mentals of natural circulation and how temperature variations in thermophysical properties
complicates modeling it.
Next, part of the experimental record for molten salt natural
circulation loops, including the TAMU facility this work targets, along with the thermo-
physical property data needed to interpret it. That is followed by a selection of previous
computational studies of such loops, along with meshing choices that control discretization
error. Then, literature outlining the reduction from full conjugate heat transfer to thermal
boundary conditions followed by the V&V methodology adopted for this work along with
a selection of digital twin applications that consume validated models. Lastly, the chapter
concludes by stating the reseach gaps this thesis addresses.
