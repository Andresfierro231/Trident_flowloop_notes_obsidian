The material uncertainty does not include uncertainty for any solid materials contained
within the rcExternalTemperature. The insulation uses properties from the manufacturer’s
datasheet fit with a second order polynomial. Only five data points exist and only one is
within the applicable range of experimental temperatures, but without more data and the R2
value of the fit exceeding 0.99, its associated uncertainty has been ignored despite being the
most likely source of unaccounted error. The other solids modeled (SS-316 and quartz) have
negligible thermal resistances by comparison and are generally well-characterized, so their
uncertainty is also neglected. Water properties also being well-posed, leaves the properties of
HITEC as the only remainging uncertainty; with standard uncertainties from Section 5.2.2
that have been propagated with the exception of density, whose uncertainty is already carried
by the back-calculation of the experimental mass flow from the PIV data in the previous
chapter. Poiseuille pipe flow scaling laws are also used for this propagation to velocity and
mass flow rate. For the propagation to temperature uncertainty, the property uncertainties
are applied to the heat transfer equation Q = ˙mcp∆T.
The environmental uncertainty shares a similar propagation path, but rather than ac-
counting for the insulation’s uncertainty, uses the first order model to characterize the un-
certainty from the ambient temperature, external convective heat transfer coefficient, the
emissivity of the insulation and heat exchanger assembly’s aluminum exterior, as well as
the measured uncertainty associated with the facility’s heater power. These quantities are
combined with a root-sum-square before being quantified below and further propagated.
The final three rows combine each contribution into the root-sum-square for the value
of uinput to be used for each QoI for each of the eight experimental cases. The temperature
entries are applied equally to both centerline and wall temperature probes.
