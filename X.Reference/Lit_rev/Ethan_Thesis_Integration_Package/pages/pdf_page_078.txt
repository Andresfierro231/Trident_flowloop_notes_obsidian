Diagnostic Fields
The boundary condition computes and stores per-face diagnostic fields:
• qsrc: heat source contribution (from heater power Q)
• qconv: external convection heat flux
• qrad: external radiation heat flux
• qfluid: heat flux from fluid into wall
• qbalance: residual (should approach zero at steady state)

Solution Strategy
The simulations use a pseudo-transient approach iteration, using adjustable time-stepping to
stay within the range of stability below a maximum Courant number of 5. This configuration
sacrfices temporal accuracy for computation speed towards steady state, but for an instance
such as this where we are only interested in the steady-state of the simulation, there is no
loss in simulation fidelity. Attempts were made to use a strictly steady-state solver approach,
but were ultimately thrwarted by oscillatory behavior resultant from the sensitive nature of
natural convection and the stagnant initial condition, a better initialized or pseudo-transient-
seeded initial condition may result in stable convergence using a steady-approach, but has not
been tested at this time. These pseudo-transient simulations were run until they sufficiently
converged to steady state. A case was determined to be at steady-state when, over the last
