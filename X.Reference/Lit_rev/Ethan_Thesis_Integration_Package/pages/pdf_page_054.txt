While not an inherent flaw in the experimental results, it does introduce an additional
source of uncertainty for modeling efforts such as this one and attempting to differentiate
between methodology differences, rounding errors, or other unknowns when trying to recreate
reporting methodologies exactly can be difficult.
The equations reported by Janz et al. for density and thermal conductivity do not have
any clear, modern superiors so have been retained for the present analysis. Curiously, Sohal
et al. states there are no correlations for the thermal conductivity of HITEC despite it being
listed alongside the other properties and equations reported in Janz et al. [26]. Reis uses a
correlation reported by Santini et al. 1984, which is also used for this work [48].
The modern suite of thermophysical properties chosen for this study is outlined in Chapter

• Specific heat: cp ≈1423.47 J/(kg K)
• Thermal conductivity: k ≈0.547 W/(m K)
