4. To perform systematic code verification using multiple mesh refinement levels to con-
duct grid convergence studies, using Richardson extrapolation and the Grid Conver-
gence Index (GCI), following the procedure of Celi´k et al. [12] to ensure not only that
the results presented here are accurate and reliable, but to also provide high-quality
data for the continued Digital-Twin efforts for advanced nuclear systems developed at
the University of Texas at Austin.
5. Lastly, the ultimate goal of this work is to fully validate the simulation pipeline against
all eight experimental test cases from Reis et al. [9], comparing simulated temperatures,
time-averaged velocity profiles, and mass flow rates against experimental measure-
ments, with the goal of replicating those results within 1σ of uncertainty or otherwise
determining the source of any remaining discrepancy in the results.

Thesis Outline
The remainder of this thesis is organized as follows:
• Chapter 2 reviews the historical and modern literature regarding natural circulation
loop experiments and simulation, CFD simulation methods, especially pertaining to the
simulation of closed-loop natural circulation, molten salt thermal hydraulics, material
properties of molten HITEC salt and water, and V&V methodology.
• Chapter 3 presents the methodology used from beginning to end of the V&V pipeline.
In this chapter the structure of the pipeline is displayed along with the procedure for
using the pipeline. This procedure includes a summary of the configuration file struc-
ture, integration and discussion of how the first order model works and is implemented
to the simulation pipeline from the experimental data, along with the implementation
of Richardson extrapolation, the Grid Convergence Index, and the validation error
assessment procedure.
