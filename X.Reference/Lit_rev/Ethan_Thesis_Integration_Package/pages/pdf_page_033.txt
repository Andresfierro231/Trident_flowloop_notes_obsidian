2.7
Summary and Research Gap
This survey of the literature reveals several key gaps that this thesis addresses:
1. Limited CFD Validation of Molten Salt Systems: The experimental facility at
Texas A&M provides the most comprehensive velocity profile dataset for molten salt
natural circulation loops along with it’s thermocouple readings, yet no independent,
three-dimensional CFD simulation has been systemically Verified and Validated against
this data.
2. Insufficient Boundary Conditions short of full CHT: OpenFOAM’s stock bound-
ary condition externalTemperature assumes a slab geometry for heat flux through
surfaces assigned that boundary condition and for cylindrical pipes with thick insula-
tion, the area ratio between the inner and outer surfaces makes this a poor approxi-
mation that would otherwise need to be resolved via more complex CHT modeling.
3. Difficulty with Energy Balance Closure: Previous experiments such as Reis et
al. have large uncertainties associated with the heater power applied to the system
not being equal to the calculated rate of heat removal by the system’s cooler, making
it impossible to accurately model the full system without better representing all the
heat losses throughout the system.
4. Accounting for Entry Length Effects: Depending on the geometry of the system
and the working fluid studied, there can at times be cases where the fluid never fully
develops in the classical sense for neither its thermal or momentum entry lengths. These
