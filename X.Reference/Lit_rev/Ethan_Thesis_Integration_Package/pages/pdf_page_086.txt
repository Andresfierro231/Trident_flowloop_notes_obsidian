Chapter 6
Results
This chapter presents the verification and validation results. Following the V&V method-
ology of Chapter 3, verification results are presented first (Sections 6.1-6.2), followed by
validation against experimental data (Section 6.4).
6.1
Boundary Condition Verification
The custom rcExternalTemperature boundary condition was verified on a simplified single-
pipe geometry by comparing the integrated wall heat flux against analytical reference values.
6.1.1
Verification Geometry
The verification domain is a vertical cylinder with an O-grid mesh:
• Inner radius: r0 = 10.0 mm, diameter D = 20.0 mm
• Wall layers: SS-316 (1.0 mm, k = 16.3 W / m K) and insulation (50.0 mm, k =
0.144 W / m K)
• Outer radius: r2 = 61.0 mm, area ratio r2/r0 = 6.1
• Adiabatic entry length: Ladiab = 500.0 mm
