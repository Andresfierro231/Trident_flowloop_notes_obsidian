4.2
Instrumentation
The following section outlines the instrumentation that was used on the loop to collect the
data for this V&V study.
4.2.1
Temperature Measurements
The thermocouples that were used were K-type thermocouples that had a reported un-
certainty of 1.1 ◦C for individual measurements, as reported by the manufacturer, and an
uncertainty of 1.56 ◦C for differences in temperature as calculated by the experimentalists
and reported in Reis et al. [9].
• Fluid probes (TP1-TP6): The centerline temperature probes of the "TPX" series
were nominally centered within the pipe’s cross section at the reported locations in
each of the four corner junctions of the loop plus two more in the fittings that mounted
the top and bottom of the test section to the rest of the loop.
• Wall thermocouples (TW1-TW11): The wall temperature probes of the "TWX"
series were placed on the outside of the loop approximately equidistant from each other
along each of the four legs. The exact axial and azimuthal locations have been lost
since the conduction of the experiment, so the comparisons conducted in this study
are for their nominal axial locations and record data at each of the cardinal directions
at the nominal axial point in space. Notably, TW10 is an exception to the trend of
the other wall thermocouples, as it was mounted on the exterior of the heat exchanger
jacket rather than the primary loop’s pipe wall.
