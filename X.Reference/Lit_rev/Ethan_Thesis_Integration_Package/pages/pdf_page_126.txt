in T [K] by Horner’s method, lowest degree first. As with air, the table is transposed so that
the coefficients read down the rows.

Density and viscosity track IAPWS-IF97 to well below the standard’s own ±0.05% un-
certainty band across the full liquid window. Thermal conductivity and specific heat drift
upward at higher temperature, reaching roughly 0.4–0.5% at the top of the operational
range, which indicates their fits were tightest near ambient. Both remain well inside the
experimental PIV uncertainty (1.4–2.1%) for every operating condition in this study.
