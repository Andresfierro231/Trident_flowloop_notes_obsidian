The internal film resistance accounts for the thermally developing boundary layer between
the bulk fluid and the pipe inner wall. With Gz = Re Pr D/Ltest = 334 × 53.2 × 0.02/0.1 =
3554, the correlation of Equation (3.13) gives NuD = 26.67, yielding an internal convection
coefficient of hint = NuD kf/D = 720W / m2K. The three resistances are:
• Rint = 1/hint = 0.00139 m2K / W (internal film; only 0.93% of total)
• Rps = 0.11902 m2K/W (cylindrical wall conduction, dominated by the 50 mm insula-
tion layer)
• Rext = 1/(ar hext) = 0.02939m2K/W (external convection, where ar = r2/r0 = 6.1 is
the area ratio)
While the internal film resistance contributes less than 1% of the total, it is still necessary
to account for, otherwise the GCI will have a near 1% error that always remains. For Mode B
(radiation only) and Mode C (mixed), the outer surface temperature was obtained by Newton
iteration on the nonlinear energy balance at the outer surface, using Reff = Rint + Rps as the
total resistance from bulk fluid to outer surface.
