For each mode, the analytical reference heat flow is computed using a thermal circuit ap-
proach (Figure 3.1) for which the verification case was designed to best-match the assump-
tions a thermal circuit would use. Those assumptions are:
• Axisymmetric Geometry: both sections of pipe are straight, co-linear, and cylin-
drical.
• Constant Centerline Temperature: the non-adiabatic region being examined is
short and the flow is fast, resulting in a negligible ∆T from the region’s inlet to outlet.
• Steady-State Flow: with constant boundary conditions, there is no surviving d/dt
after the flow develops
• Constant Material Properties: the simulation and circuit use constant properties.
• Constant Environmental Conditions: the ambient temperature T∞, convective
heat transfer coefficient h∞and environmental emissivity (assumed equal to 1), are set
