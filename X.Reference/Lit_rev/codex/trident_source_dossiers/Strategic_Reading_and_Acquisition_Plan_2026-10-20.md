# Strategic reading and acquisition plan for the two TRIDENT reviews

Search and planning date:20October2026. The governing brief remains Markdown collé.md and its62question matrix. The priority order below is our decision-value judgment, not a citation-count ranking or a completed novelty finding.

## What will advance the reviews fastest

Complete a small linked evidence chain:

1. **Engineering need:** which circuit/mission needs which temperature, duty, circulation or response prediction?
2. **Representativeness:** which mechanisms a glycol/salt natural-circulation experiment can establish, and which source/property/geometry distortions remain?
3. **Inference:** which states/parameter combinations the actual observations support?
4. **Adequacy:** which retained physics changes the declared prediction enough to matter?
5. **Independent assessment:** what data and uncertainty checks can support the claim?

Each reading should resolve one of these decisions and feed both a source dossier and a connected synthesis paragraph. Broader discovery is useful when it challenges a specific candidate claim. We should avoid adding method families without feasible observations or a comparison they can decide.

These findings narrow what we can claim. They do not invalidate a carefully specified new result for loop transport, observation-aware reduction or independent predictive adequacy.

## How to avoid wasting the next pass

For every source, record one consequential question, exact evidence/limits, and which paragraph/table changes. Stop a detailed extraction when the decision is already resolved or its assumptions clearly prevent transfer; retain a scoped coverage statement. Keep discovery leads separate from established results.

The next synthesis should compare competing explanations:

- poor excitation versus structural non-identifiability;
- uncertain boundary/sensor model versus omitted heat transport;
- physical state enrichment versus a fitted correction;
- good calibration replay versus independent future prediction;
- reactor scaling versus a mechanism demonstration.

Do not expand the experiments until a comparison says what information they must add. Do not turn an unobserved local-flow hypothesis into a core deliverable by assuming CFD or new instruments.
